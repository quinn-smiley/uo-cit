Services

Gel X

According to Allure, one benefit of Gel-X is easy removal. Additionally, gel requires less agressive preparation and removal. According to Essence, do to the lack of filing required, the risk of inflamed nail beds is reduced. They also do not emit the same chemicals as acrylics do. Gel-X is a healthier way to get nail extensions overall.

Acrylic

According to Allure, acrylics are beneficial in that they do not require an LED lamp to cure and they can be filled regularly without getting an entirely new set. LED lamps can cause skin damage and sunscreen is advised when using these lamps. Additionally, according to Essence, some people have found acrylics to be less likely to cause allergic reactions or inflamtion.
